# 3D Valley

Stardew Valley mod that renders the game's entire world in three dimensions and lets you explore it from a freely movable camera or a first-person view

Everything under the surface is the original game. Farming, foraging, mining, fishing, crafting, combat, relationships, quests, festivals, and seasons all run on Stardew Valley's own systems, because 3D Valley is a new way of seeing the valley rather than a remake of it

It needs nothing beyond Stardew Valley and SMAPI, runs fully offline, and never modifies your saves.

No other engine, runtime, library, tool, or supporting mod is required

> [!WARNING]
> 3D Valley is in active development and is not playable yet
> 
> Expect missing content, unfinished worlds, and breaking changes
